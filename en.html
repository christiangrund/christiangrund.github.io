Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Controller

Christian Grund, address and email as in the legal notice.

2. In short

  • The game stores your save data on your computer.
  • The full game has online features: the bag holding your finds, leaderboards, conquest, the community list and online co-op. For these it signs in to our server at startup, with a random ID and, if Steam is running, with your SteamID.
  • Publicly visible are your Steam display name with your results on leaderboards and territories, and the names of challenges you publish.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics services, no selling of data.
  • The demo has no online features and sends us nothing.

3. On your computer

Save data, settings and the sign-in for the online features are kept in the game’s storage on your computer. This is strictly necessary for the game (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG) and needs no consent. If the game crashes, it writes an error report there as well; it is never sent. If you play the full game on Steam with Steam Cloud turned on, Steam keeps a copy of your save data in your Steam account and syncs it between your computers; Valve’s privacy policy applies to that. You can turn Steam Cloud off per game in the Steam client.

4. Online features of the full game

What we process:

  • a random player ID that the game creates on first start;
  • your SteamID and Steam display name: the game hands the server a sign-in ticket from Steam, the server verifies it with Valve and asks Valve for your display name. This way your progress follows you to every computer you play on;
  • your progress on the server: coins and eyes, finds, vessels, equipment and their bookings;
  • runs the game submits: the recording of your inputs, result, form, sector, time, kills, level and game version. The server replays them so that only real runs appear on the leaderboards;
  • conquest: territories, attacks and defences;
  • challenges you publish: the name you give them, their setup and your best time;
  • technical data of every connection: IP address, time, requested function and error messages, in our host’s logs.

Why and on what legal basis:

  • to provide the online features you use in the game (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR);
  • to keep leaderboards fair, prevent abuse and run the server reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is a fair, working game);
  • to improve the game: we analyse submitted runs in aggregate, for example how far each form gets or where runs end, never to assess individual players (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is a balanced game).

Public: leaderboards, territories and the community list show your Steam display name together with your results; without Steam you appear as “SPIELER” (German for player) with six characters of your ID. The game takes the name from Steam at every start. To appear under a different name, change your display name in Steam.

How long: as long as your online account exists. Recordings of solo runs are deleted once they have been verified; the result stays on the leaderboard. The host deletes logs containing IP addresses after seven days at the latest.

Without the online features: without an internet connection the game keeps running locally, then without the server-side bag, leaderboards, conquest and online co-op.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore) runs the database and server for us as a processor (Art. 28 GDPR). The database is located in Frankfurt am Main at Amazon Web Services. For operations and support, Supabase may transfer data to Singapore and the USA, based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).
  • GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA) runs the automatic verification of submitted runs for us. It processes the recordings with the player ID, not the SteamID or name. Transfer basis: EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) and Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Valve (Valve Corporation, USA) verifies the Steam sign-in ticket and gives us your display name, see section 6.

6. Steam

Achievements, stats, invites and lobbies, the online co-op connection and the overlay run through Steam. Valve processes data for this as an independent controller under its privacy policy. From Valve we receive your SteamID, your display name and confirmation that you own the game.

7. Online co-op

  • Through a Steam invite the connection runs over the Steam network. Steam may also connect the two computers directly; the other side then technically learns your IP address.
  • Through a room code our server introduces the two computers, after which they connect directly; here too the other side learns your IP address. To find a path through routers, the game queries STUN servers from Google (stun.l.google.com, stun1.l.google.com; Google LLC, USA) and Cloudflare (stun.cloudflare.com; Cloudflare Inc., USA), which see your IP address.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, since you start the co-op session. Transfer to the USA: EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

8. Links from the game

Discord, the Steam page and this page open in your browser. The privacy policies of those providers apply there (Discord Inc., Valve, GitHub).

9. This page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical details to deliver and secure the page (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; transfer: EU-US Data Privacy Framework). The page sets no cookies, loads nothing from third parties and counts no visits. GitHub privacy statement.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You may object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can lodge a complaint with any data protection authority, for example the one where you live (Art. 77).

Deleting your online data: write to christiangrund.info@gmail.com and give your SteamID (17 digits, in Steam under account details). Within one month we delete everything the server holds about you: ID, SteamID, progress and finds, runs, leaderboard entries, territories and the challenges you published. So that nobody can have someone else’s data deleted, we may ask you to confirm that the Steam account is yours. If you start the game with an internet connection afterwards, it creates a new online account. You remove the save data on your computer in the game under Settings, Save data, “Delete progress”.

11. Changes

If the game changes, we update this policy. The date above applies.

Report content

The community list shows challenges under names that players choose themselves; leaderboards show Steam display names.

Rules: no illegal content, no insults, hate or threats, no personal data of others, no advertising. Anything that breaks these rules is removed from the list.

To report: write to christiangrund.info@gmail.com, subject “Report”, with the name of the challenge or player, where you saw it and why you consider the content illegal or not allowed (Art. 16 Digital Services Act). We review every report promptly and reply to you.

Point of contact for authorities and users under Art. 11 and 12 Digital Services Act: the same address, in German or English.
